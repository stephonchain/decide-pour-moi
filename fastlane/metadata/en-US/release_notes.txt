A new look, the same wheel.

• A fresh design in the colors of the icon: a night background, a single yellow for actions, and every color saved for the wheel.
• Results now appear at the bottom of the screen: the wheel stays visible behind, and “Spin again” sits right under your thumb.
• A “Spin the wheel” button joins the flick and the hub: one tap is enough.
• “My wheels” gets a makeover: bigger tiles, and anything locked is visible before you tap.
• The free version now includes two wheels: “Tonight we eat…”, editable three times, and “Heads or tails”, now open to everyone.
