Stop hesitating. Build your wheel, spin it: decided.

Who does the dishes tonight? Pizza or sushi? Which movie? Who goes to the board? Who plays first? Some decisions don't deserve a ten-minute debate. Let the Wheel Decide settles them in five seconds, with a smile.

YOUR WHEEL IN THIRTY SECONDS
Type or paste your list: one line becomes one option, that's it. Paste your class's twenty names or the six restaurants nearby — the wheel is ready. Reorder with a drag, duplicate a wheel, share it as plain text.

TRULY RANDOM DRAWS
The result is drawn by iOS's cryptographic random generator before the wheel even starts spinning: the animation only reveals it. No bias, no tricks possible. The wheel itself spins like a real one — momentum from your flick, natural slowdown, a little tick on every segment, confetti at the finish.

THREE DRAW MODES
• With replacement: the drawn option stays on the wheel. The classic.
• Without replacement: each draw removes its option until none are left. Perfect for handing out chores with no repeats.
• Running order: the wheel chains draws and builds an ordered list by itself. Who goes first, second, third… then share the list in one tap.

MADE FOR CLASSROOMS
Teachers get it instantly: paste your student list, pick “running order”, and calling someone to the board becomes a little ritual instead of a dreaded moment. The draw is fair, visible to everyone, and nobody can blame anybody.

GIVE YOUR CRAVINGS BETTER ODDS
Any option can weigh two or three times more than the others: its slice grows, and so do its chances. Perfect for leaning gently toward pizza without slamming the door on salad.

ALSO INSIDE
• Five ready-made wheels: Yes / No, Heads or tails, Numbers 1 to 10, Who goes first?, Tonight we eat…
• History of each wheel's last fifty draws
• Six matching color palettes
• Interface in English and French, switchable inside the app

YOUR DATA STAYS WITH YOU
No account, no tracker, no third-party ads. Your wheels, options and draws are stored on your iPhone and never leave it. The privacy policy fits on one page, and you can read it offline inside the app.
